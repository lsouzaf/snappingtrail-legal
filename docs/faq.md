The questions a new account asks in its first weeks. Each answer is short; the link goes into the details. For the Collect app's own questions, see [its FAQ](./collect-app/faq.md).

## I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page, use the password recovery option. It sends you an email to set a new one.

## An interviewer left the company.

Deactivate them under **User Management**: they can no longer sign in, and their interviews and reports stay. A user who took part in an interview cannot be deleted. See [Deactivate and reactivate a user](./administration.md#deactivate-and-reactivate-a-user).

## The survey's link stopped taking answers.

The respondent sees why. The link may have expired or been disabled, the survey may be paused, the survey or the link may have reached its response limit, or your plan's allowance may have stopped link collection. See [When a link can't take answers](./collection.md#when-a-link-cant-take-answers).

## The Collect app did not send the interviews.

Sending is automatic, as soon as there is a connection. On **Pending Interviews**, a **Not sent** interview is tried again by itself, and the sync icon sends everything at once. If the app went back to the sign-in screen, sign in again: the interviews are still on the device. See [Sending interviews](./collect-app/sending.md).

## Why does a finding show as an indication?

The difference is there, but the sample is not yet large enough to be sure. Watch it as more answers come in. See [Firm or indication](./results.md#firm-or-indication).

## Why don't I see the differences between segments?

They are part of the Pro plan; the trial shows them too. On Free and Starter, the screen says how many findings Pro would add. A segment also needs at least 5 respondents in the group and in the rest of the sample. See [What each plan shows](./results.md#what-each-plan-shows).

## A delivery rule did not send anything.

Open **Delivery History**. **Nothing new in the period** means there was nothing to send. Otherwise, the **Details** column says what the destination answered. The first run of an interview list or responses rule only marks where the next one starts. See [Delivery history](./integrations.md#delivery-history).

## How do I change my plan?

An administrator does it under **Settings**, in **Billing & Plan**. See [Change or cancel the plan](./administration.md#change-or-cancel-the-plan).

## What happens when the trial ends?

You have 7 more days to choose a plan, still with every Pro feature. After that, the account goes on with the Free plan's features and limits. See [The trial](./administration.md#the-trial).

## How do I export all my data?

Under **Settings**, **Customer History** has the full export: the usage, the activity timeline and every interview's answers, in a file to download. See [Export the account's history](./administration.md#export-the-accounts-history).

## How do I close the account?

Ask support. See [Legal](./legal.md) for the contacts.
