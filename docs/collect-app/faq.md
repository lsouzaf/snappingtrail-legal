# Frequently asked questions

## I forgot the company code. How do I get it?

The code comes from the company that runs your survey: ask your company's account administrator. SnappingTrail support cannot recover it.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the sign-in page that opens in the browser, use the password recovery option ("Forgot password"), which sends you an email to set a new one.

## I lost the connection in the middle of an interview. Did I lose the answers?

No. Each answer is saved on the device as you go, with or without a connection. Finish the interview normally: it is kept on the device and sent by itself when the connection returns, on every plan.

## Do I need to press anything to send my interviews?

No. They are sent by themselves whenever there is a connection. The sync icon on **Pending Interviews** only sends them right away (see [Sending interviews](./sending.md)).

## The app sent me back to the sign-in screen.

Your session expired ("Your session has expired. Sign in again."). Sign in again: your interviews waiting to be sent are still on the device.

## The app is showing old surveys, or a new survey is missing.

Pull the **Projects** list down to refresh it. If the survey is still missing, check with your company's account administrator that it is active and that you are part of its project.

## The app got slow or is behaving strangely.

Close the app fully (swipe it away from recent apps) and open it again. If it persists, restart the device. If it still persists, contact support.

## I need to change devices, or uninstall the app. What about my interviews?

Interviews not yet sent exist **only on this device**, and they are not included in the device's backups. **Uninstalling the app deletes them.** Before uninstalling or changing devices, open **Pending Interviews** with a connection and check that nothing is left waiting to be sent. Signing out, on the other hand, keeps them.
