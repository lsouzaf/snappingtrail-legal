# Collecting responses

## 4.1 Choosing a project

On the Projects screen, tap the project you will work on. Each project has one or more active surveys.

## 4.2 Choosing a survey

Inside the project, tap the survey you want to apply. The app loads the questions and shows the collection screen.

## 4.3 Answering questions

Each question can be of a different type:

- **Single choice** — the respondent picks one option.
- **Multiple choice** — the respondent can pick several options.
- **Free text** — you type in the answer dictated by the respondent.
- **Numeric scale or rating** — the respondent picks a number (e.g., 0 to 10 in NPS).

Use the **Previous** and **Next** buttons to navigate between questions. Answers are saved automatically on every change — you do not need to press "save" for each question.

## 4.4 Finishing the interview

On the last question, tap **Finish**. The interview goes into the sending queue.

## 4.5 Resuming an interrupted interview

If you were interrupted mid-interview (phone call, device crashed, respondent had to leave), the app saves the draft automatically. When you come back, you will see the option **Continue last interview** — tap to resume exactly where you left off.
